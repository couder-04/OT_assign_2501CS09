
ASSIGNMENT  —  TRANSPORTATION PROBLEM  (VAM + MODI)

========================================================================
PROBLEM STATEMENT
========================================================================
  Three factories ship to four warehouses.
  Unit transportation costs, supplies and demands:
+--------+----+----+----+----+--------+
|        | W1 | W2 | W3 | W4 | Supply |
+--------+----+----+----+----+--------+
|   F1   | 19 | 30 | 50 | 10 |   7    |
|   F2   | 70 | 30 | 40 | 60 |   9    |
|   F3   | 40 | 8  | 70 | 20 |   18   |
+--------+----+----+----+----+--------+
| Demand | 5  | 8  | 7  | 14 |        |
+--------+----+----+----+----+--------+
  Total supply = 34  =  total demand = 34   (balanced)
  Objective: minimize total transportation cost.

========================================================================
(i) VOGEL'S APPROXIMATION METHOD  —  Initial Basic Feasible Solution
========================================================================
  At each step:
    1. Penalty of a row/column = (2nd lowest cost) - (lowest cost)
    2. Choose the row or column with the largest penalty
    3. Allocate as much as possible to its cheapest available cell
    4. Cross out the exhausted row or column and repeat

  Column penalties: {'W1': 21, 'W2': 22, 'W3': 10, 'W4': 10}
  Largest penalty is COLUMN W2 = 22
  Cheapest cell in this column: (F3, W2) cost 8

========================================================================
(ii) MODI METHOD  —  Optimality test and iterative improvement
========================================================================
  For every basic cell (i, j):   u_i + v_j = c_ij
  For every non-basic cell:      d_ij = c_ij - u_i - v_j
  MIN problem is optimal when every d_ij >= 0.
  If some d_ij < 0, enter the most negative cell, form a closed
  loop with basic cells, and shift theta = min of (-) positions.
